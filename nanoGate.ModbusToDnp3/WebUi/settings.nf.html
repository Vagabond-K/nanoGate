Auto transceiver: the module controls DE/RE by itself and no pin is used. Native RTS: the MCU drives the DE/RE pins of a MAX485/MAX3485-style chip.

One rule connects a range of consecutive Modbus addresses in an area to a DNP3 point block at once. A count of 1 is a single-point mapping, and the scale (x numerator / denominator + offset) converts raw values to engineering units. Each rule specifies the RS-485 slave (Unit) it polls. Rules apply after saving and rebooting.
